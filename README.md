# NaduCare – Kerala Community Problem Reporting Platform

**NaduCare** is a web-based community platform designed to help people report local problems in Kerala, track their progress, and contribute to improving their neighborhoods. It connects citizens and administrators through a simple and transparent problem-reporting system.

##  Features

- **Report Local Problems:** Submit issues such as road potholes, damaged streetlights, waste accumulation, drainage blockages, and water supply problems.
- **Problem Details:** Add a title, description, location, category, and supporting images.
- **Browse Problems:** View reported community issues in one place.
- **Search and Filter:** Find problems by category, location, or status.
- **Track Progress:** Monitor the status of reported problems.
- **User Authentication:** Allow users to register, log in, and manage their reports.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Manage reported problems and update their statuses.
- **Image Uploads:** Upload and display problem images using Cloudinary.
- **Responsive Design:** Access the platform from desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

##  How It Works

1. A citizen registers or logs in.
2. The citizen submits a local problem with its description, location, category, and image.
3. The reported problem becomes available for browsing.
4. An administrator reviews the report and updates its status.
5. Citizens can revisit the report and follow its progress.

##  Example Problems

- Potholes on local roads
- Broken streetlights
- Overflowing public waste bins
- Blocked drainage systems
- Water supply interruptions
- Damaged public infrastructure

##  Project Objective

The main objective of NaduCare is to encourage community participation, improve visibility into local problems, and provide a more organized way to report and monitor civic issues across Kerala.
